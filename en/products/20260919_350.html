About this piece

Unique Handmade Ceramic Statement Piece for the Home Vase for Fresh Flowers This is not just a vase for dried flowers. It is a pomegranate with attitude — mysterious, slightly wild, and absolutely one of a kind. Hand-sculpted from ceramic, this pomegranate has a deep dark surface covered with a delicate network of light crackle lines, creating the feeling of aged stone or dry earth. And then comes the surprise — a spectacular glossy deep-red pomegranate crown, flowing dramatically down the sides like drops of red lacquer. The contrast between the dark textured body and the vivid red crown makes this piece impossible to overlook. Fill it with dried flowers, grasses, delicate branches or seed heads — or leave it empty and let the pomegranate itself become a small piece of sculptural art in your home. A simple natural twine and handmade ceramic tag complete its rustic, artistic character. ✨ Entirely handmade. ✨ One of a kind — no two pieces can ever be exactly alike. ✨ A beautiful statement piece for a home, studio, creative space, or a truly unusual handmade gift. Not just a vase. A little piece of ceramic art with a wild heart.

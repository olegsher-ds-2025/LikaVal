About this piece

The little Christmas tree has a secret: she is never alone. By her side is her faithful little friend — a snowman. She can chat with him about how her day went, laugh with him about something silly, or simply sit quietly together, watching the warm candlelight glow. He never interrupts, never argues, and never says, "I told you so!" 😊 And when darkness falls outside, a gentle light begins to shine from within the tree, and it feels as though a little Christmas fairy tale is coming to life right there beside you. Perhaps this is exactly the kind of friend your holiday evenings have been missing?

About this piece

This little fish was born from clay, texture, color, and the desire to bring a small piece of the sea into the home. Hand-sculpted from ceramic, it is covered with layers of turquoise, sea-blue, white and silvery tones. The body is full of handmade textures, impressions and tiny details that make the surface wonderfully tactile and impossible to look at only once. I especially love the fins — richly textured and highlighted with dark lines that make every detail stand out. A small handmade ceramic shell above the fish completes the story, as if this little creature has just returned from the sea and brought a treasure with it. This is a one-of-a-kind handmade ceramic wall decoration, perfect for a coastal home, creative studio, nursery, beach-inspired interior, or anyone who loves unusual handmade art. No two handmade pieces are ever exactly alike — and this fish cannot be recreated in precisely the same way. A little piece of the sea, shaped by hand. And perhaps she already knows where she is going.

About this piece

This little horse was shaped from clay, warm hands and a touch of fairy-tale magic. She has a graceful long neck, a deep dark-red mane and matching tail, and tiny white daisies blooming across her warm terracotta-colored body. I wanted her to feel simple, gentle and alive — a little character to enjoy from every side. Each side has its own small story. This is a one-of-a-kind handmade ceramic sculpture, created individually by hand. A charming piece of handmade art for a home, nursery, creative space, or for someone who loves horses, nature and the warmth of handmade ceramics.

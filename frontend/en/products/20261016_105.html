About this piece

A Living Fairy Tale by Soul Ceramics A LIVING FAIRY TALE ABOUT A LITTLE PENGUIN WHO DIDN’T KNOW WHAT HE WAS BORN FOR The little Penguin wasn’t born like ordinary penguins. He hadn’t hatched from an egg. And he had no parents who had kept an egg warm until a tiny baby emerged from it. He was born from FIRE. Very hot fire. 🔥 The little Penguin lived on a warm wooden shelf, alongside others just like him — little creatures born from fire: a little Owl, a Bear, a Hedgehog, and many other adorable little ones. It was warm and cozy there. But one day, the Penguin overheard a story about penguins living in Antarctica, a place filled with ice and snow. He began asking his neighbors what this ice and snow actually were. But not one of them had ever even heard of such things. He searched for ice in the nearby mugs standing on the shelf, peered into every bowl, and even looked inside the sugar jar. But there was no ice and no snow anywhere. The truth was, he didn’t even really know what he was looking for. At last, he quietly sat down on the edge of the wooden shelf and let out a heavy sigh. And just at that moment, a pair of warm hands gently lifted him up. “Oh, aren’t you just adorable…” The little Penguin didn’t say a word. He only felt how warm and cozy he was in those hands. And suddenly, he understood something very important. Not every penguin has to live where there is ice and snow. Some are born to bring warmth to people. 🐧 About this little character Meet an adorable handmade ceramic penguin figurine, lovingly created in my Soul Ceramics studio. With his soft sandy-colored body, black-and-white face, tiny shiny feet, and wonderfully earnest expression, this little fellow has a personality all his own. He is a one-of-a-kind ceramic creation, shaped and finished by hand. Small details and subtle variations make him truly unique. More than just a decorative figurine, he is a little character with a story waiting to unfold — and perhaps a new home where his own fairy tale can begin. A thoughtful gift for penguin lovers, ceramic art collectors, animal figurine enthusiasts, or anyone who appreciates handmade treasures with a heart.
